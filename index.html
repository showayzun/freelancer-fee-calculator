Formulas (per platform): platform fee = gross × platform% payment fee = gross × payment% + payment fixed (min applied where noted) after fees = gross − platform fee − payment fee withdraw fee = withdraw fixed FX loss = (after fees − withdraw fee) × FX% (only if "convert" = yes) pre-tax = after fees − withdraw fee − FX loss tax reserve = pre-tax × tax% net take-home = pre-tax − tax reserve % lost = (gross − net) ÷ gross
